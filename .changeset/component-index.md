`linked build` writes a package's component index to `lib/esm/data/components.ttl` (step **Writing components.ttl**, right after `Writing shapes.ttl`): the linked components exported from `src/components/index.ts`, generated by the package's `@_linked/react` (`utils/componentIndex`). No `components/index`, no file; `--skip-shape-check` skips it too.
